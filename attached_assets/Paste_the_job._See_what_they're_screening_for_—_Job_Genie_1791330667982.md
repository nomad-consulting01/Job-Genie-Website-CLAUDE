## Why we ask for the posting first

Because it costs you nothing. A job posting is public, it is already in your history, and it says nothing about who you are.

Your CV is the opposite. Asking for it before you have any reason to trust us is the wrong order, and it is the order almost every tool in this category uses.

You can read the brief, close the tab, and apply what you learned to your own CV by hand. That is a legitimate outcome and it costs you nothing.
