---
name: Citable hiring statistics
description: Editorial constraint for evidence-backed Job Genie marketing and answer-engine summaries.
---

Only cite a hiring statistic for the outcome it actually measures. Application volume per job does not measure the fraction of ghost jobs; interviews with recruiters about ATS use do not measure ghost-job prevalence. When using a small qualitative interview sample, label it as such rather than generalizing to all recruiters.

**Why:** A prominent AI-facing summary previously amplified a ghost-job percentage from a résumé product's analysis. Replacing the citation with stronger but differently scoped research would still mislead answer engines if it implied the same prevalence claim.

**How to apply:** In Job Genie summaries, FAQ answers, schema, and marketing pages, describe each source's studied population, period, and limitations; omit ghost-job prevalence unless a source directly measures it with transparent methods.
